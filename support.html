Help with Tellurion

Questions, bugs and ideas are all welcome while the app is in beta.

Write to it.msm88@gmail.com

It helps to include your iPhone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of the me screen) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

How do I share a program?

Open the program in the editor, tap the … menu at the top right and choose share. Tellurion creates a file you can send by message, mail or AirDrop, or save to Files.

How do I open a program someone sent me?

Save the file to your iPhone, then on the today screen tap import and pick it. The program opens in the editor so you can check it before you start.

How do I keep my training log safe?

Your log lives only on your phone. Go to me → back up my sessions to save it as a file, somewhere such as iCloud Drive. To bring it back, on this phone or a new one, use me → restore from a backup. Restoring never overwrites or duplicates sessions already on the phone.

Can I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Yes, in me → units. Your logged weights are converted when you switch.

Some exercise pictures are blank

The pictures are downloaded when a screen shows them, so they need a connection and can occasionally fail to load. Everything else in the app works offline.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. To stop sharing anonymous counts, switch off me → help improve Tellurion; that also deletes the random ID on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

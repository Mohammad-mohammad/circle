What Tellurion does with your data

Last updated 15 October 2026. Applies to the Tellurion app for iPhone (previously called Circle) and to this website.

In short: Tellurion has no accounts. Your programs and training log stay on your phone. If you choose to, the app sends four anonymous counts so we can see which features are used. Exercise pictures are loaded from another company's server.

Who is responsible

Tellurion is made by Mohammad Mohammad, an individual developer, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: it.msm88@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

  • The name you give the app, your weight unit and, if you enter it, your body weight.
  • The programs you create or import, including any cover photo you pick.
  • Your training log: sessions, sets, weights and reps.
  • Your settings, such as muscle colors.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Tellurion has no cloud backup, so we cannot restore it for you.

Files you choose to share

When you share a program, or back up your training log, Tellurion creates a file and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice, and it is handled by the app or service you send it to. A backup file contains your training log and the programs it belongs to. It does not contain your name.

Anonymous usage counts (optional)

The app asks once whether you want to help improve Tellurion. This is off unless you say yes, and you can change it at any time in me → help improve Tellurion.

If it is on, the app sends a count each time one of four things happens:

  • a program was created
  • a program was shared
  • a program was imported
  • a session was finished

Each count carries only the name of the event, the time, a random ID, the app's version, and the fact that it came from an iPhone. It never includes your name, your programs, your exercises or your weights. The random ID is created on your phone when you opt in and is not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device.

Switching the setting off deletes the random ID and any counts not yet sent. Switching it on again creates a new ID.

The counts are processed for us by PostHog on its EU cloud. We have configured the project so that IP addresses are not stored with the counts, and location lookup is disabled for every count. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by switching the setting off.

Exercise pictures

The animated exercise pictures are not stored in the app. They are downloaded from ExerciseDB (static.exercisedb.dev) when a screen shows them. As with any download, that server receives your IP address and which picture was requested. We do not receive this information and do not control how that provider handles it.

What Tellurion does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, location, health data or microphone.

Tellurion opens your photo library only when you choose a cover photo for a program, and the photo stays on your phone.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or the app. Anonymous counts are kept in aggregate form for as long as they are useful for improving the app. Because they are not linked to you, we cannot pick out or delete an individual person's counts after they have been sent.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK or a place with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. In practice almost everything Tellurion holds is on your own phone, under your control. For anything else, write to the address above.

Children

Tellurion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects what the app sends, the app will ask for your consent again before anything new is sent.

a workout app for trainers and the people they train

Build a training program as an orbit. Record every set.

Epicycle lays a program out the way you think about it: the week in the centre, days around it, exercises around each day. Then it gets out of the way while you train: today's day sits in the middle with its exercises around it.

in beta for iPhone · not yet on the App Store

create

A whole program on one screen

Add weeks, set which days you train, and place exercises around each day. Choose the reps and the rest for every set. Each muscle group has its own color, so you can read a week at a glance.

share

Send it as a file

A program is one file. Send it to a client or a training partner by message, mail or AirDrop, and they open it in Epicycle with the weeks, days, sets and rest times exactly as you built them.

train

Tap a set, save it, rest

Today's day sits in the middle with its exercises around it. Tap a set, enter reps and weight (steps of 1, with quarters), and save: the rest timer starts in the same card. Every session is saved to your calendar, so you can see what you lifted last time.

Yours, on your phone

No account and no sign-in. Your programs and your training log are stored on your iPhone, and you can back the log up to a file whenever you like.

Private by default

Epicycle sends nothing about your training anywhere. Anonymous usage counts are off unless you switch them on. Read the privacy policy.

Free during the beta

The beta is free. Building programs, sharing them as files and recording sessions will stay free after it.
